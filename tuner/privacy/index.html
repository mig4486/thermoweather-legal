Privacy Policy

Effective 15 October 2026 · tgstartup

This policy explains how Tunelo for iOS, developed by tgstartup, handles information. It covers the app and these public support pages.

1. Microphone and information on your device

The tuner uses microphone input only to detect pitch on your device. We do not save recordings or upload microphone audio. Microphone access is requested after an explanation and can be changed in iOS Settings.

App preferences, instrument and tuning choices, custom tunings and favorites, saved tempos, song setlists, practice duration and notes, and your weekly practice goal are stored locally. The app has no user accounts or developer-operated cloud sync. You can export your completed practice journal as CSV using the system share sheet; you choose the receiving app or destination. The developer does not receive this export. Removing the app removes its local data; any device backups are governed by your Apple settings.

2. Optional Firebase Analytics

Usage analytics collection stays disabled until you explicitly allow it. Before the first onboarding step, a choice screen shows a preselected preference, which does not itself enable collection. You can continue without analytics or change your choice later in Settings. If you allow analytics, Google Analytics for Firebase may process:

  • Predefined interactions: onboarding, microphone permission result, tool usage, the first successful tuning in an audio session, practice duration in minutes, help articles and paywall interactions.
  • Built-in instrument/tuning identifiers, metronome tempo and meter, selected subscription product, purchase outcome and confirmed Pro status.
  • SDK-generated app-instance identifiers and technical information such as device model, operating system, app version, language, session timing and approximate country/region. Google describes the handling of network metadata in its policies.
  • Verified App Store purchase metadata, including the product, value, currency and transaction information needed for subscription measurement.

We do not send audio, practice notes, custom tuning/song names, email addresses or free-form error messages as analytics parameters. The app does not set a personal user ID. Advertising identifiers (IDFA/IDFV), ad personalisation and advertising consent are disabled in this integration. The iOS app does not display ads.

We use these measurements to understand activation, use of tools, reliability of purchase flows and subscription conversion. We do not use them for personalised advertising or sell personal information. Google processes analytics information through Firebase/Google Analytics under its service terms and may process information outside your country.

Read Firebase privacy information and Google’s Privacy Policy.

3. Purchases and Apple

Subscriptions are purchased through the App Store. Apple handles payment and billing information; the developer does not receive your card details. The app asks Apple to verify active subscription entitlements and reads store prices and trial eligibility. This access check works independently of optional analytics.

Technical transaction identifiers may be kept locally to avoid reporting the same purchase twice. They are passed to the SDK’s StoreKit verification API rather than included in custom analytics fields. Refunds, cancellation and renewal are managed by Apple. See Apple’s Privacy Policy.

4. Your choices, retention and deletion

You can turn analytics off in the app’s Settings. The app then disables collection and resets locally held Firebase analytics data. Turning analytics off does not remove practice records and does not automatically delete events already received by Google.

Local preferences and practice records remain until you edit/delete them or remove the app. The local transaction ledger remains while the app is installed to prevent duplicate purchase reporting. User/event-level analytics records follow the Google Analytics property’s configured retention settings; aggregated reports may remain. See Google Analytics retention controls.

Where applicable law provides these rights, you may request access, correction or deletion, restrict or object to processing, request portability, withdraw consent or complain to your data protection authority. Contact us using the address below. We may need additional information to locate SDK-generated records because the app has no personal accounts. Withdrawal does not affect processing already carried out with consent.

5. Support, children and these pages

If you contact support, we process your email address and message to answer the request, investigate the issue and meet applicable obligations. Do not send passwords or payment-card information. Support correspondence is retained as needed to handle the request and satisfy applicable law.

If you believe a child provided information without consent required by law, contact us to request review and appropriate deletion. Analytics can be left disabled.

These pages contain no analytics scripts or advertising. They are hosted by GitHub Pages; GitHub may process request logs under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

6. Contact and changes

Developer: tgstartup. For privacy or support requests, email tgstartup@icloud.com.

We will update this page when app features or data processing change. The effective date appears above. Any material new analytics or advertising functionality will require an updated disclosure and, where required, consent.
