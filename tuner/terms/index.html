Terms & Subscription Information

Effective 15 October 2026 · tgstartup

These pages explain use of Tunelo for iOS and its Pro subscriptions. Developer: tgstartup.

1. App licence

The application is licensed under Apple’s Standard Licensed Application End User License Agreement. That agreement governs the app licence and applicable rights. This page describes the app’s subscription operation; it does not replace the Apple agreement or mandatory consumer rights.

2. Free and Pro features

The app provides tuning, metronome and practice tools. Pro unlocks the features described on the purchase screen. Weekly and annual plans provide the same Pro access for their respective subscription periods.

Pro access is activated only after the App Store confirms an active entitlement. Cancelling automatic renewal does not normally end access before the paid period expires. Ending Pro does not delete your local practice notes or settings; Pro features become unavailable when the entitlement expires.

3. Prices, free trial and renewal

The current local price, currency, billing period and any eligible offer are displayed in the app and Apple’s purchase confirmation. Apple determines trial eligibility. When offered, the free trial lasts 3 days. A different plan does not automatically provide another free trial.

If you accept a trial and do not cancel automatic renewal, it converts to the selected paid subscription at the price shown before purchase. Weekly plans renew weekly; annual plans are charged for and renew for a year. Any displayed annual saving is calculated against 52 weekly charges in the same currency, using the current store prices.

Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled through Apple before renewal. To avoid a charge, follow Apple’s cancellation instructions and cancel at least 24 hours before a free trial ends. Apple displays and governs the final billing and cancellation terms.

4. Manage, restore and request a refund

Manage or cancel a subscription in iOS Settings → your Apple Account → Subscriptions. See Apple’s cancellation instructions. Use “Restore purchases” in the app with the Apple Account used to purchase the subscription to check eligible access.

Refund requests are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com, subject to its rules and applicable consumer law. The developer does not receive your payment-card details or control Apple’s billing decisions.

5. Local information and support

Preferences, practice notes and saved tempos are kept on your device. The app does not provide developer-operated cloud sync. Read the Privacy Policy for microphone handling and optional Firebase Analytics.

For app or subscription-access problems, email tgstartup@icloud.com. Include the app version and a description of the issue; do not include passwords or payment-card details.
