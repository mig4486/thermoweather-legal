1. The service

ThermoWeather shows third-party weather forecasts and brings together temperature values from external Bluetooth sensors and manual entry. The acoustic method and household-signs guide provide experimental or approximate room-air estimates only. The iPhone itself does not expose a sensor that measures room-air or body temperature in degrees. Supported features also include weather maps, air-quality and other environmental information, ventilation and outdoor-drying recommendations, a lunar calendar and northern-lights viewing conditions. The app has no user account or social feed requirement.

2. Accuracy and intended use. Forecasts are probabilistic third-party estimates. Room temperature should come from an external BLE sensor or a separate thermometer entered manually. The acoustic method is experimental and is not a calibrated thermometer; the household guide gives rough estimates only, and the iPhone heat indicator is not air temperature. The app is not a medical device and does not measure body temperature with iPhone sensors: body values are entered manually and do not replace a medical thermometer or professional advice. Do not rely on the app as the sole data source where measurement or forecast errors could cause harm to life, health or property.

Acoustic estimates. Results depend on microphone/speaker routing, entered distance and geometry, reflections, background noise and humidity. Use the setup instructions and supported routes. An uncertainty value is a model estimate, not a validated guarantee of absolute accuracy. Missing humidity uses a disclosed assumption; outdoor humidity is not a measurement of indoor humidity.

Ventilation, moisture and drying. Recommendations are estimates based on available measurements and forecasts. Indoor recommendations require appropriate room data; a weather forecast alone does not measure your room. A suggested drying window does not guarantee when laundry will dry, and ventilation guidance does not diagnose or remediate mold or replace building, health or safety advice. Recheck conditions before acting, especially for future intervals.

Maps, northern lights and calendar. Weather layers show the model and time identified in the app, not necessarily observations. Aurora activity is probabilistic; actual visibility also depends on darkness, cloud cover, viewing position and local conditions. A future calendar date may have astronomical information without an available aurora map or weather forecast for that night. A map for the nearest time is not a forecast for every selected date. Lunar events and times are estimates for the selected place. Official warning feeds have their own coverage and update delays; check the responsible authority for urgent decisions.

Timers and reminders. Notification delivery depends on system permissions, settings and device operation. Do not rely on a notification as your only safety measure. A running timer does not operate a window, fan or other appliance.
