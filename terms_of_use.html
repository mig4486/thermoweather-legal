1. The service

ThermoWeather shows third-party weather forecasts and brings together temperature values from external Bluetooth sensors and manual entry. The acoustic method and household-signs guide provide experimental or approximate room-air estimates only. The iPhone itself does not expose a sensor that measures room-air or body temperature in degrees. Supported features also include weather maps, air-quality and other environmental information, ventilation and outdoor-drying recommendations, a lunar calendar and polar-aurora viewing conditions. The app has no user account or social feed requirement.

2. Accuracy and intended use. Forecasts are probabilistic third-party estimates. Room temperature should come from an external BLE sensor or a separate thermometer entered manually. The acoustic method is experimental and is not a calibrated thermometer; the household guide gives rough estimates only, and the iPhone heat indicator is not air temperature. The app is not a medical device and does not measure body temperature with iPhone sensors: body values are entered manually and do not replace a medical thermometer or professional advice. Do not rely on the app as the sole data source where measurement or forecast errors could cause harm to life, health or property.

Acoustic measurement. The main 1.1.0 flow uses the built-in speaker and supported microphones, without requiring a wall, and records a local measurement session. A legacy echo method, where available, is separate. Device support, audio routing, preparation, geometry, noise, humidity and calibration affect whether a valid result can be obtained. Accuracy on iPhone has not been established as a calibrated room thermometer; any displayed uncertainty is a model estimate, not a validated error bound. The flow may return recordings, quality results or an unsupported/invalid result rather than a temperature. Use an external thermometer for a reliable reference. Local audio storage and optional export are described in the Privacy Policy.

Ventilation, moisture and drying. Recommendations are estimates based on available measurements and forecasts. Indoor recommendations require appropriate room data; a weather forecast alone does not measure your room. A suggested drying window does not guarantee when laundry will dry, and ventilation guidance does not diagnose or remediate mold or replace building, health or safety advice. Recheck conditions before acting, especially for future intervals.

Maps, aurora and calendar. Weather layers show the model and time identified in the app, not necessarily observations. Aurora activity is probabilistic; actual visibility also depends on darkness, cloud cover, viewing position and local conditions. A future calendar date may have astronomical information without an available aurora map or weather forecast for that night. A map for the nearest time is not a forecast for every selected date. Lunar events and times are estimates for the selected place. Official warning feeds have their own coverage and update delays; check the responsible authority for urgent decisions.

Timers, threshold alerts and aurora reminders. Delivery depends on permission, operating-system scheduling and device operation. Threshold alerts require the selected supported sensor and fresh readings; a disconnected sensor is not continuous monitoring. Indoor recommendations cannot establish current room conditions from missing or stale measurements. Aurora reminders use forecast conditions refreshed while the app is active and schedule local notifications; they are not a continuous storm-detection or emergency service. A timer does not operate a window, fan or appliance.

Aurora history and seasonal planning. Kp is a planetary activity index, not a guaranteed local sighting probability. NOAA map percentages describe the model at its stated time and location, rather than a personal chance of seeing aurora. Historical observations and seasonal cloud/darkness/activity summaries can help compare places and months, but do not predict that an event will repeat. Long-range activity outlooks and weather forecasts have different coverage and uncertainty; missing dates are not filled by treating current conditions as future observations.

Astronomical events and cameras. Event dates, local visibility and translations are informational and may be corrected through catalog updates. A global event is not necessarily visible from your location, and an announced event does not guarantee a live broadcast. Do not look directly at the Sun through unfiltered optics or without appropriate solar-viewing protection. Camera availability, image freshness, stream quality and operator schedules vary. Still images and panoramas are identified separately from continuous video. Sources may stop, change their player or display independent advertising. Plus does not guarantee uninterrupted access to third-party media.

5. Sharing results and acceptable use

Where available, sharing exports the selected supported result as an image or text after your action. Review its city, dates, units, source and freshness before sharing. A saved reading or forecast snapshot is not continuously updated after export. A drying/ventilation card does not start a timer or reminder for its recipient or transfer a Plus entitlement. Sharing a Plus-only recommendation requires access when the card is prepared and sent. The receiving app and any recipient handle exported copies under their own terms; deleting a local card cannot recall them.

Acoustic session exports can contain raw audio, device/session metadata and reference readings. Export only recordings you are entitled to share. The app does not automatically upload these files.
