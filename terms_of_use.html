1. The service

ThermoWeather shows third-party weather forecasts and brings together temperature values from external Bluetooth sensors and manual entry. The acoustic method and household-signs guide provide experimental or approximate room-air estimates only. The iPhone itself does not expose a sensor that measures room-air or body temperature in degrees.

2. Accuracy and intended use. Forecasts are probabilistic third-party estimates. Room temperature should come from an external BLE sensor or a separate thermometer entered manually. The acoustic method is experimental and is not a calibrated thermometer; the household guide gives rough estimates only, and the iPhone heat indicator is not air temperature. The app is not a medical device and does not measure body temperature with iPhone sensors: body values are entered manually and do not replace a medical thermometer or professional advice. Do not rely on the app as the sole data source where measurement or forecast errors could cause harm to life, health or property.
