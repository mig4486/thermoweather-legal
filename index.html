ThermoWeather Support

Help with indoor temperature tools, weather forecasts and ThermoWeather Plus.

Contact us

For questions, bug reports or suggestions, email tgstartup@icloud.com.

To help us understand a problem, include your app version, iOS version, iPhone or iPad model, and the steps that led to it.

Common questions

How do I get room temperature and humidity?

Connect a compatible Bluetooth Low Energy temperature and humidity sensor, or add a manual reading from a separate thermometer in the Thermometer tab. The iPhone itself does not directly measure room temperature. The acoustic method is an experimental estimate; its accuracy depends on the room and measurement conditions.

My sensor is not connecting

Check the sensor battery, keep it near your device and make sure Bluetooth is on. In iOS Settings, check that ThermoWeather is allowed to use Bluetooth. Support depends on the sensor model and firmware. If it still does not connect, email us the model and firmware version, if known.

I purchased Plus, but the features are locked

Use Restore Purchases on the Plus purchase screen or the subscription screen in the app's Settings. Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple Account used for the purchase and have an internet connection. Apple's purchase restoration guide.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

On your iPhone or iPad, open Settings → your name → Subscriptions, then select ThermoWeather. You can also open Apple subscription management. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. Apple's cancellation guide.

The weather is not loading or looks out of date

Check your internet connection and selected city, then refresh the forecast. You can choose a city manually without giving location permission. Some data sources have limited coverage, and not every location has marine, pollen or official warning data. If the problem continues, contact us with the affected screen and the time of the problem.

The aurora map does not match my selected night

The aurora layer has a shorter forecast horizon than the calendar. Check the map's timestamp. If no map exists for a future night, the app may offer the nearest available map separately; the selected night's conditions remain a distinct view. Clouds and darkness also affect visibility.

How do sharing and spoken weather work?

In builds that offer them, tap Share on a supported saved result and review the preview before choosing an app or copying text. Shared cards are snapshots and do not set up a timer for another person. Tap the speaker next to the city to hear a short weather summary; tap again to stop. Neither action starts automatically.

How do I change my privacy choices?

In version 1.0.17 and later, open Settings → Privacy → Your privacy choices to change optional usage analytics and crash reports. Advertising privacy choices and iOS permissions are managed separately. See the Privacy Policy for details.
